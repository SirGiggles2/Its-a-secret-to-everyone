### Advisor 1: The Archivalist (NES Source Authority)

To achieve byte-perfect dungeon parity, we must treat the NES Z1 source as the absolute authority for OAM generation. The primary mechanism for enemy and item drawing on NES is the **ObjAnimations** index table (`Z_01.asm:1958`) and the **ObjAnimFrameHeap** (`Z_01.asm:1977`). These tables resolve a logical `ENEMY_TYPE` or `ITEM_ID` into a specific sequence of tile IDs and attribute bytes. We must ensure that our Genesis port's `k_obj_animations` and `k_obj_anim_frame_heap` in `src/game/world/draw_dispatch.c:73-150` are 100% synchronized with the NES values. Every enemy update, from the wandering Stalfos (`enrt_update_stalfos`) to the complex Goriya boomerang trajectory, eventually calls `DrawObjectWithAnim`, which consumes these heaps. Any deviation here results in non-perfect animations.

A critical missing link for UW perfection is the per-level palette source. On NES, the palette is not room-specific but level-constant, loaded into **LevelInfo_PalettesTransferBuf** (`Variables.inc:330` at RAM `$6B7E`) during the dungeon entry sequence (`InitMode2Load` in `Z_06.asm`). This 32-byte block contains both the BG and SPR sub-palettes ($3F00-$3F1F). Currently, `src/game/dungeon/uw_render.c` fallbacks to searching captured "blobs" via `load_palette_from_levelinfo`, which is imprecise. We must instead source the palette directly from the `nes_ram` copy of the LevelInfo block installed by `level_info_install_uw`.

Finally, boss parity requires faithful replication of the over-Link priority handling found in `Z_04.asm`. Bosses like Aquamentus and Gleeok use specialized draw paths that bypass standard OAM cycling to ensure they render above Link or with specific scanline priorities. We must audit the `enemy_render.c` cache to ensure that `enemy_render_publish_gleeok` and similar specialized publishers preserve the exact OAM slot order used by the NES for these multi-segment entities.

**Effort Estimate:** 6h (Table verification + LevelInfo palette integration).

### Advisor 2: The Bridge Builder (Genesis Architect)

Our strategy relies on the "multi-latch cache" bridge in `src/game/enemies/enemy_render.c`. This layer emulates the NES OAM mirror by capturing every `anim_write_sprite` call during the `enemy_loop_tick`. To reach byte-perfection, we must refine the `translate_tile` and `translate_attrs` logic (`enemy_render.c:487-540`) to handle the Genesis-specific normalization of NES bank switching. Specifically, the **UWSP** (Underworld Sprite) banks must map correctly to the `ROOMROM_SCENE_OBJ_TILE_BASE` in VRAM. The current mapping at `enemy_render.c:510` correctly handles common sprites vs. transient bank sprites, but we must verify the `NES_OWSP_BANK_FIRST (0x8E)` threshold against every dungeon's specific sprite set.

For palettes, the Genesis 4-palette limit is bridged via **subpal_routing**. Our `roomrom_bg_palette_load_palram_full` (`src/game/world/bg_palette.c:43`) maps NES SPR sub-pal 1 (Blue ramp) to Genesis `PAL2` and sub-pal 2 (Red ramp) to `PAL3`. This allows red Octoroks and blue Darknuts to coexist with Link (`PAL1`). We must ensure that item drops (hearts/rupees) use the **ITEM_ATTR_MARKER** (`enemy_render.c:215`) to route through the `k_nes_item_tile_to_atlas_idx` lookup, ensuring their tile IDs map to the correct indices in the Genesis item atlas.

Door animations (explosions/sparkles) must be wired through the **Meta-object** system. When a bombable door explodes, we must trigger `enemy_render_publish_meta` with the "cloud" frame tiles. Currently, `src/game/dungeon/door_state.c` only patches tiles; it needs to spawn Type `$5D` (DeadDummy/Cloud) objects at the doorway coordinates. This ensures the "puff" animation matches the NES frame-for-frame during the transition.

**Effort Estimate:** 10h (Meta-object wiring + Item atlas sweep + Boss priority audit).

### Advisor 3: The Validator (Quality Assurance)

Verification is the only path to finality. We will extend the `cave_golden` infrastructure into a new **uw_golden** suite. This suite will exercise all 18 dungeon entries (L1-L9 x Q1/Q2) by forcing a warp to the `start_room` of each, then iterating through every room ID in the `rooms_dungeons` block. For each room, we will capture the final OAM state, the VRAM hash of the sprite tiles, and the CRAM values. These will be compared against a "Golden Baseline" generated from the NES authority (using a modified NES emulator or the `roomrom_uw_redux_toggle.lua` probe).

A "Byte-Perfect PASS" is defined as:
1. **OAM Parity:** Every sprite slot (0-63) matches NES Y, Tile, Attr, and X after Genesis coordinate normalization (X+8, Y+32).
2. **CHR Parity:** Every tile ID used in OAM exists in the Genesis VRAM atlas and matches the NES 2bpp source bit-for-bit (after 4bpp expansion).
3. **PAL Parity:** The colors in PAL0-PAL3 match the NES PALRAM source, with Link always locked to PAL1.

We must pay special attention to bosses with multi-segment logic like **Gleeok** and **Patra**. Their segments are not standard objects and require capturing the `enemy_render_entry_t` cache directly. The CI sweep should also verify the "dark room" palette transition in L9 (Ganon) to ensure the palette cycles for the Triforce flash match the NES `LevelInfo_PaletteCycles` table (`Variables.inc:345`).

**Effort Estimate:** 8h (uw_golden tool development + 18-dungeon sweep + CI integration).

**Total Effort Estimate:** ~24h (3-4 development days).
